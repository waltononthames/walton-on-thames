# User experience plan, November 2026

**Status:** proposed 10 November 2026. Not yet adopted. No site content or code was changed to produce it.
**Owner:** Darren. **Scope:** how the site works for the people using it, on a phone first.
**Companion documents:** `docs/improvement-plan-2026-11.md` (search), `docs/directory-plan.md`, `docs/hersham-head-term-plan.md`. Where an item already exists in one of those it is referenced, not repeated. IDs such as B14 are that plan's backlog IDs.

This plan asks a different question from the search plan. That one asks what would help the site rank. This one asks what gets in a visitor's way once they have arrived. The two agree more often than not, but the order differs: the homepage rebuild and outreach, first and second there, are seventh and absent here.

## What this is based on, and what it is not

| Evidence | Detail |
|---|---|
| Measured on the live site, 10 November 2026 | A phone-sized browser (390 by 844, and 360 by 740 for the width check) on twelve key pages: screenshots of the first two screens, positions of the menu button, the first heading and the first useful link |
| Measured on the full build at `3a4f250` | Every indexable page parsed: links, headings, images, page length. Width checked on all 420 pages |
| Lighthouse, live site | 22 pages, phone profile: speed and accessibility |
| Not done | No person was watched using the site. No analytics were read. No screen reader was used. No desktop review beyond what the build shows |

So this is a list of measured friction, in the order a visitor would meet it. It is not a usability study. Section 5 says how to get one cheaply.

Three quarters of search visits are on phones (the Search Console figure quoted in the brief for the search plan), so every finding is stated for a phone unless it says otherwise.

## 1. Principles

1. **Answer first.** A page's first screen should hold the thing the visitor came for, or a visible way to reach it. The Apps Court status panel is the model.
2. **A page must fit the screen it is read on.** No sideways scrolling, no shrunken text, the menu always reachable.
3. **No dead ends.** Every page links onward to the next thing a visitor on that page would want: the venue from the event, the events from the venue, the history from the visit page.
4. **One page per thing.** Two pages about the same place that disagree cost more trust than a missing page.
5. **Long is fine if it can be scanned.** Headings, a contents list and a way back to the top matter more than word count.
6. **Honest state.** Dated facts, "what we checked", and a status that is never stale. Already the site's strongest habit; keep extending it.
7. **Accuracy rules still apply.** Nothing here licenses unverified content. Each item names its editorial standard where it touches copy.

| Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|

Good, with exceptions. Of 22 live pages, 16 show their main content in under 2.5 seconds on a simulated mid-range phone. `/history/sidney-road/` takes 6.6 seconds because of two very large images. Five photo pages take about 3.

## 3. What to change, in order

Ordered by how many visitors are affected and how badly, then by cost. **C** is Claude in the repository, **D** is Darren.

| # | Change | Why first | Effort | Who | Also in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | Make every page fit a phone screen | 52 pages, including the busiest, lose their menu and clip their text | S | C | B14 |
| U2 | Shorten the title block on phones, and put the Apps Court status directly under the title | Half of every first screen is spent before content starts | S | C | New |
| U3 | Events link to their venue; venue guides list their next events | 58 of 97 events are at two venues that have guides | M | C | B9 |
| U4 | Homepage: buttons and section tiles on the first screen, paragraph after | A first-time visitor sees no way forward | S | C | Part of B1 |
| U5 | Remove dead ends: merge Xcel, link the three orphans, link the paired pages both ways, redirect the two old addresses | Each is a visitor who stops | S | C | B10, B12, B37 |
| U6 | Directory: keyword box, filters kept in the address, description and address shown once | The longest page on the site, with the weakest way in | M | C | Directory plan 2.1, 2.2 |
| U7 | Contrast, tap-target sizes, padding on the search page | Affects every page a little | S | C | B16 |
| U8 | Headings on the fourteen pages without any; a contents list and "back to top" on pages over about twenty screens | Scanning and screen-reader use | M | C | New |
| U9 | Images: declared sizes and smaller versions for phones | Six slow pages | M | C | B15 |
| U10 | A check that fails the build when a status file is out of date | Before any second status page | S | C | B25 |
| U11 | Ask a few people to try real tasks | The only way to learn what this plan cannot see | S | D | Directory plan 6.2 |

Left out on purpose: a redesign, a live map with markers (directory plan principle 7), an "open now" filter (principle 6), reviews or ratings (principle 5).

## 4. Phased checklist

In the form of `docs/hersham-head-term-plan.md`. Take the next unticked item in the lowest incomplete phase. Where an item is also in another plan, tick it in both.

### Phase 1: the page fits and the answer shows (one session)

- [ ] **1.1 Width (U1).** As the search plan's Phase 1.1. Done when a phone-sized browser reports no indexable page wider than 360 pixels, and the menu button's right edge is inside the screen on every page.
- [ ] **1.2 Title block (U2).** Reduce its padding and the size of the lead paragraph below about 600 pixels wide, across the shared templates. Target: on a 360 by 740 phone the first line of body content starts in the top half of the screen on article, guide and listing pages. Check at 320, 360 and 390 wide.
- [ ] **1.3 Apps Court status (U2).** Move the status panel above the introductory paragraph. No wording changes. Done when the panel's headline is visible without scrolling at 360 by 740.
- [ ] **1.4 Search page padding (U7).** Give the search page's content the same side padding as every other page.
- [ ] **1.5 Contrast and tap targets (U7).** Footer text to a passing contrast ratio. Card title links to at least 24 pixels tall. Re-run Lighthouse accessibility on the 22 pages and log the scores.

Gate: `npm run check`, `npm run build`, `npm run seo:validate`, `npm run seo:links` clean; screenshots at 360 by 740 of the homepage, Apps Court, a history article, a listing and What's On saved alongside the build-log entry.

### Phase 2: no dead ends (one to two sessions)

- [ ] **2.1 Events to venues (U3).** The venue line on an event page links to the venue's guide, listing or club page wherever one exists. Start with Riverhouse Barn and the Playhouse.
- [ ] **2.2 Venues to events (U3).** A "What's on here" block on the Riverhouse Barn and Playhouse guides, reusing the block on `/hersham/`. Content Verification Protocol: the block shows only events already in the collection.
- [ ] **2.3 Xcel (U5).** Search plan Phase 1.3.
- [ ] **2.4 Orphans and pairs (U5).** Search plan Phase 1.4.
- [ ] **2.5 Old addresses (U5).** Search plan Phase 1.10.
- [ ] **2.6 A signal on the chip rows** that they scroll: a fade at the right edge, or let them wrap.

Gate: no page without an inbound link other than closed listings; every event with a venue page links to it.

### Phase 3: the homepage and the long pages (two sessions)

- [ ] **3.1 Homepage first screen (U4).** Shorten the hero paragraph to two or three sentences and place the buttons and section tiles directly under it, with the fuller description moved below the tiles. This is a layout change to existing verified text. If the search plan's homepage rebuild (its Phase 2) is going ahead, do this as part of it and not twice. **[Darren decides which]**
- [ ] **3.2 Headings (U8).** Add section headings to the fourteen pages that have none. Labels only, describing what is already there.
- [ ] **3.3 Contents and back to top (U8).** A contents list at the head of `/things-to-do/` and the long history articles, as `/living/` has, and a small "back to top" control on pages over about twenty screens.
- [ ] **3.4 Images (U9).** Search plan Phase 1.8.

### Phase 4: the directory (follows the directory plan)

- [ ] **4.1** Directory plan 2.1: listing page, with the description, address and phone shown once.
- [ ] **4.2** Directory plan 2.2: keyword box, filters in the address, result count.

### Phase 5: find out what this plan cannot see

- [ ] **5.1 Read the analytics** **[Darren]**. Cloudflare Web Analytics is running on the live site. From its dashboard, note the ten most visited pages, the share of visits on phones, and the Core Web Vitals it reports. Save the figures in the build log.
- [ ] **5.2 Five task tests** **[Darren]**. Ask three to five people, on their own phones, without Google, to: find out whether the car boot is on this Sunday; find a café to work in near Hersham Green; find what is on at Riverhouse Barn this weekend and where to park; find a GP surgery taking new patients; find when Walton Bridge was built. Note where each hesitates or gives up. Fifteen minutes each.
- [ ] **5.3 Re-rank this plan** against 5.1 and 5.2, and remove anything they do not support.
- [ ] **5.4 Status check (U10)** before any new status page.
