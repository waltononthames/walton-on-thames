## 9. For groundhoppers: the Swans' story

Walton & Hersham are known as the Swans. The club's own history page tells the full story; here are the milestones.

1895
Walton FC was formed.
1945
After the Second World War, Walton FC amalgamated with Hersham FC to form Walton & Hersham FC.
1973
On 14 April 1973 the club won the FA Amateur Cup at Wembley, in front of 41,000, with a late goal by Roger Connell against Slough Town.
2017
At the end of September 2017 the club left Stompond Lane, its home for eight decades, when the ground was sold for development, and moved to the new Sports Hub on Waterside Drive.
2026
The first team plays in the National League South in 2026-27.

The club's history page: waltonhershamfc.com/club/history. Our local history section covers the town the club grew up in.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

What is the postcode for Walton & Hersham FC?

The Elmbridge Xcel Sports Hub is on Waterside Drive, Walton-on-Thames, Surrey KT12 2JP. Use that postcode for your sat-nav.

What is the nearest station to Walton & Hersham FC?

Walton-on-Thames. The ground is on the far side of town, beside the river, so the walk takes about 58 minutes by road. A taxi or Uber takes about 7 minutes.

Is there parking at the Xcel Sports Hub?

Yes, there is a free car park on site. Accessible parking is available.

How much are tickets at Walton & Hersham FC?

For 2026-27: adults £16, seniors (65 and over) and concessions £13, ages 13 to 17 £8, ages 12 and under £3. Pay on the gate by cash, card or contactless, or buy in advance on the Fanbase app. Disabled supporters pay the concession price, with up to one free carer ticket.

What time do matches kick off?

Saturday home league matches kick off at 3pm and Tuesday evening matches at 7.45pm. A few kick off at other times, so check the club's fixture list.

Is there a bus from Walton-on-Thames station to the ground?

No. The 564 bus runs from Church Street in the town centre to the Xcel Leisure Centre stop, every hour at 13 minutes past, 9.13am to 6.13pm. The last bus back from the Xcel leaves at 6.23pm, and there are no buses in the evening or on Sundays.

What time is the last train from Walton-on-Thames to London Waterloo?

About 12.36am on Saturdays and 11.50pm on weekday evenings, in the timetable that runs until 12 December 2026. Engineering work can change this, so check live times before you travel.

Where can away fans drink before the match?

The club recommends The Weir, on the towpath a short walk from the ground. Both walking routes from the station also pass pubs, listed in walking order in our pubs chapter.

Can I walk along the river to the ground?

Yes. Our river route follows the Thames Path for about 1.1 km and takes about 57 minutes from the station, against about 58 minutes by road.

Is the ground accessible for disabled supporters?

Disabled supporters pay the concession price, with up to one free carer ticket. Accessible parking is available.
