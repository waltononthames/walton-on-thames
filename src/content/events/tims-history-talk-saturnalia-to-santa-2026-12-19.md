Tim Hazell, Riverhouse Barn's Historian in Residence, explores the pagan roots of Christmas: how gift-giving, pine trees, Santa Claus and reindeer came to be part of the festive season, and the long journey the holiday took to become the one celebrated today.

The talk runs from 7:30pm to 8:30pm and tickets are £10.
