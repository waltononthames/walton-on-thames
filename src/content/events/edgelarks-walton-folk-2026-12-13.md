Walton Folk's December date brings Edgelarks to Riverhouse Barn. The venue describes the BBC Folk Award winning duo of Phillip Henry and Hannah Martin as combining "British folk roots with diverse influences from blues to Indian ragas", having toured from Canada to South Africa and Australia to Japan.

Tickets are £18 and the lunchtime concert runs from 12.15pm to 2.15pm.
