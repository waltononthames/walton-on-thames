Love Walton returns to Bridge Street for a second year with a day built around tasting what the street has to offer: its cafes, restaurants and pubs, alongside local food producers and other businesses in the town centre's historic conservation area.

The organisers' timetable for the day runs as follows:

- 11am: the event opens, with food, drink and local produce on offer
- 12pm to 4pm: free face painting, glitter tattoos and balloon modelling
- 2pm to 3pm: The Kimberley Grae Band on stage on the driveway of The Bear pub
- 2.30pm to 3pm: Beer Can Bubble Show outside The George Inn
- 3.45pm to 4.15pm: Beer Can Bubble Show outside The George Inn
- 3.45pm to around 4.45pm: The Kimberley Grae Band on stage again
- 5pm: the event closes

The live entertainment and children's activities are free. Charges apply for food, drink and stall produce.
