Oliver Darley and Chad LeLong, the team behind Simply Bowie, Simply George Michael and Simply Freddie, bring an intimate evening of songs from the Great American Songbook, performed with voice and piano and told alongside the stories behind them.

The programme ranges from Cole Porter to Gershwin, Berlin and Rodgers & Hart, with songs including 'Moon River', 'Summertime' and 'White Christmas'. The evening runs from 7:30pm to 9:30pm and tickets are £16.
