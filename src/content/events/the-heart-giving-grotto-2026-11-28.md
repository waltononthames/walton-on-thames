The Heart Shopping Centre opens The Giving Grotto on Festival of Light Day, Saturday 28 November, from 1pm to 5pm. Father Christmas will be in the grotto to hear festive wishes as visitors donate.

The centre is collecting new, unwanted and unopened gifts for children, women and men, which will go to local charities supporting survivors of domestic abuse, people facing homelessness and children in hospital over Christmas. Visitors can also donate to Walton & Hersham Foodbank. The centre's post lists the items the foodbank needs most this Christmas, including hot chocolate, long-life sponge puddings, tinned fish, UHT milk, toiletries and household essentials.
