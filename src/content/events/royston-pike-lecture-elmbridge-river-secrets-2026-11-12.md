The November Royston Pike Lecture, Elmbridge River Secrets, runs from Wey and Mole ecologies to Thames mudlarking adventures. It includes a 45 minute talk by Lara Maiklem, a licensed mudlark with more than twenty years' experience on the Thames and author of Mudlarking: Lost and Found on the River Thames.

The lecture runs from 7:30pm to 8:45pm.
