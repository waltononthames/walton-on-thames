James W.P. Campbell, architect, architectural historian and author of The Library: A World History, looks at how cultures around the world have designed their libraries, and what those buildings say about the societies that built them.

The talk is a tribute to Edgar Royston Pike, who started the lecture series in the late 1960s. The Royston Pike Lectures are presented by Elmbridge Borough Council with Riverhouse Barn. It runs from 7:30pm to 9pm on Wednesday 3 March 2027, and tickets are £5.
