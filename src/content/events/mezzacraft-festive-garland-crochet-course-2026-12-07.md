Crochet teacher Merrian Holland of Mezzacraft runs a two session course in Walton-on-Thames on crocheting mini socks and mittens, which are then strung on a cord to make a garland or a hanging decoration. Students follow a pattern while learning how basic socks and mittens are made.

There are two groups, each meeting on Mondays 7 and 14 December 2026:

- **Daytime**, 12:30pm to 2:30pm at Riverhouse Barn Arts Centre, Manor Road, KT12 2PF.
- **Evening**, 7:30pm to 9:30pm at St Erconwald's Church Hall, 22 Esher Avenue, Walton-on-Thames, KT12 2TA.

The course is not for complete beginners: students need to be confident with basic stitches and counting stitches. It costs £42 for the two sessions, including printed notes and video tutorials; yarn, hooks and other tools are not included. Booking is through Mezzacraft.
