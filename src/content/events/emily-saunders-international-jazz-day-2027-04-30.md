Vocalist, songwriter and producer Emily Saunders returns to Riverhouse Barn with her band for International Jazz Day 2027, mixing jazz-fusion, soul and pop with Brazilian grooves, set against animated visuals.

The show is two 45-minute sets with a 30-minute interval, starting at 8pm. Tickets are £18.
