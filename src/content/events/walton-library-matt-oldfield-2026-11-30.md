Children's author Matt Oldfield, co-creator of the Ultimate Football Heroes and Unbelievable Football series, talks about his writing, reads from his books and takes questions. It is suitable for ages 7 and over.

Tickets are £3 per child, and accompanying adults do not need a ticket. Booking is essential, online or at the library counter. Books will be on sale on the day, cash only.
