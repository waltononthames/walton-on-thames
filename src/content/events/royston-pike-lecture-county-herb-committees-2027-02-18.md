During the Second World War, County Herb Committees organised volunteers to gather and dry wild medicinal herbs while medical supplies were short. This panel discussion, chaired by Sally Flatman of the Our Plant Stories podcast, looks at what was collected and why, drawing on Surrey History Centre's records of the local campaign. It will be recorded for a future podcast episode.

The Royston Pike Lectures are presented by Elmbridge Borough Council with Riverhouse Barn. The talk runs from 7:30pm to 9pm on Thursday 18 February 2027, and tickets are £5.
