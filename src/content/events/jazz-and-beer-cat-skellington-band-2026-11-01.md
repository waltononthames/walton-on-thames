The Isle of Wight band return to Riverhouse Barn with acoustic jazz and gypsy swing drawn largely from the swing era of the 1920s and 1930s, in the style of Django Reinhardt. The line-up is Richard White on clarinet and vocals, Hugo Boswell and Steve Thompson on guitars and Jon Thorne on double bass.

The lunchtime session runs from 12:15pm to 2:15pm.
