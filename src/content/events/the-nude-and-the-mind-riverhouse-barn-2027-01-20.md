Husband and wife Constantine and Suzanne exhibit side by side, one with art nude photography and the other with life drawing, each looking at the female figure. The exhibition asks visitors to weigh up the difference between the two.

It is open daily from 10am to 4pm, from Wednesday 20 to Sunday 24 January 2027, with free admission. The venue says it is suitable for ages 16 and over at parents' discretion.
