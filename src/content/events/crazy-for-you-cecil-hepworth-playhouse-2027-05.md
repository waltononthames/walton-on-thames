Walton and Weybridge Amateur Operatic Society stage Crazy For You at Cecil Hepworth Playhouse from Tuesday 11 to Saturday 15 May 2027. The Tuesday performance runs from 7:30pm to 10:15pm, with further performances listed for the Wednesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday.

The show is a romantic comedy built on Gershwin songs, among them I Got Rhythm, Embraceable You and Someone to Watch Over Me, following a young New York banker sent to Nevada to foreclose on a rundown theatre.

Tickets are listed at £14.98 to £22.47 including the booking fee, going on sale on Tuesday 1 December 2026.
