Irish-Italian violinist Violetta Suvini, a City Music Foundation Artist for 2025 to 2027 and winner of the Royal Philharmonic Society's Emily Anderson Prize in 2024, joins Georgian pianist Salome Jordania for a Mother's Day concert at Riverhouse Barn.

The programme is Ravel's Sonate Posthume, Lili Boulanger's D'un Matin de Printemps, Dvorak's Songs My Mother Taught Me and Dora Pejačević's 'Spring' sonata. The concert runs from 4pm to 5pm and tickets are £16.
