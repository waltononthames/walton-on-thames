Crafty Pots Cafe, the pottery painting cafe at 42 The Heart Shopping Mall, holds a Sip & Paint session every Thursday evening. Arrive any time from 6:30pm; the session ends at 9pm. Entry is £6 per person plus the cost of the pottery you choose to paint on the night, and it is bring your own drinks.

Through November and December the cafe is playing Christmas music at its Thursday sessions. On Thursday 5 November it runs a one-night Oasis Sip & Paint, painting along to a playlist of the band's current tour setlist, at the same times and price.

*Sources: [Crafty Pots Cafe](https://www.craftypotscafe.com/copy-of-thursday-sip-paint-1) for the weekly session, and its [Oasis Sip & Paint page](https://www.craftypotscafe.com/oasis) for 5 November. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
