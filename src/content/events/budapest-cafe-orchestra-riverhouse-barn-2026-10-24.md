Christian Garrick and The Budapest Café Orchestra return to Riverhouse Barn after a four year absence. Established by the British composer, violinist and teacher in 2009, the group plays gypsy and folk-flavoured music in its own fresh and unconventional style.

The concert runs from 7:30pm to 9:30pm.
