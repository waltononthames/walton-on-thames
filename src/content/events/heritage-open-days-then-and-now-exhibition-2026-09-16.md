Elmbridge Museum shows historic photographs from its archives alongside modern recreations taken from the same locations, tracing how the borough has changed. The exhibition runs from 16 September to 4 October, daily from 10am to 4pm, in the Robert Phillips Gallery at Riverhouse Barn.

Entry is free and no booking is required.
