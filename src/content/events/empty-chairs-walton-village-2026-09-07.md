Empty Chairs is a simple idea: a table is booked with a few chairs left empty, so anyone wanting company can sit down, order a drink if they want one, and talk to someone. The organisers describe it as neither a club, a therapy session nor a networking event, open to anyone aged 18 to 80 and over, with no sign-up, no pressure to spend money and no obligation to stay.

The Walton-on-Thames meet-up is held at The Walton Village on the High Street from 7pm. The next date listed is Monday 7 December 2026. Look out for the orange t-shirt.
