The Elmore Quartet, a young London-based string quartet and winners of the 74th Royal Over-Seas League Annual Music Competition, give a lunchtime concert at Riverhouse Barn.

The programme is Beethoven's String Quartet Op. 18 No. 2 and Grieg's String Quartet No. 1. The concert runs from 1pm to 2pm and tickets are £16.
