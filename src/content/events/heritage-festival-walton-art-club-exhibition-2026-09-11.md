Walton Art Club fills the covered mall at The Heart Shopping Centre with members' work for the full run of Heritage Open Days, from 11 to 20 September. Artworks are available to purchase, and club members are on hand to talk about the group and how to join.

Opening hours are 9am to 5pm from Monday to Saturday, and 11am to 5pm on Sundays. Entry is free.

*Sources: [Heritage Open Days](https://www.heritageopendays.org.uk/submission-event/walton-art-club-heritage-exhibition.html) and [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/whats-on/). Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
