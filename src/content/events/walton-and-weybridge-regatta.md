The Walton & Weybridge Regatta is one of the oldest rowing regattas on the Thames, held annually since 1862 and organised by Walton Rowing Club, whose clubhouse sits on the Surrey bank of the river at Walton. It draws crews from clubs across the south of England.

The course runs around 1,000 metres, starting just below Walton Marina and finishing downstream near the Walton Rowing Club clubhouse. Spectators can watch for free from the public towpath, with refreshments typically available near the finish, between Felix Road and Sunbury Lane.

**Practical information:**
- Free to spectate from the towpath
- Nearest station: Walton-on-Thames
