Crafty Pots Cafe combines pottery painting with live Christmas music and a sing-along, with a Christmas jumper competition and a prize for the best singing table.

The cafe lists sessions on Saturday 12 December from 7pm to 9pm and Sunday 13 December from 4pm to 6pm; its Wednesday 9 December session is already full. Tickets are £18 per person plus the cost of the pottery you paint, and it is bring your own drinks and snacks.
