The community gathers on Remembrance Sunday, 8 November 2026, for the annual Remembrance Service at the War Memorial on the High Street. People are asked to gather from 10.15am. The service begins at 10.40am and is followed at 11.15am by the traditional military parade, which ends on the High Street.
