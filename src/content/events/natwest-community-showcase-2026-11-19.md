After refurbishing its branch on Walton High Street, NatWest is inviting residents and businesses to a community showcase from 5pm to 7pm on Thursday 19 November. The event is open to everyone, and food and refreshments are provided free of charge.

Visitors can pop in to meet staff from across the bank and find out about services including financial health checks, Premier Banking, business banking and digital banking support. Love Walton's listing asks people to register for the event.
