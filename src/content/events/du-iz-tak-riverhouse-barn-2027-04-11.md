Tiny Pickles present a miniature puppetry show adapted from Carson Ellis's picture book, told entirely in the secret language of insects. A group of bug friends explore their changing world and watch the seasons turn, with tiny puppets made from vintage remnants and a set built inside a suitcase.

The show lasts 30 minutes with no interval and is recommended for ages 3 to 9. The venue lists tickets up to £10.
