Walton-on-Thames Bowling Club invites members and guests to a casual roll-up game every Tuesday from 5pm, with light refreshments provided. The club says there is no need to book: "just turn up, say hello, and enjoy the game."

Newcomers do not need their own equipment, as the club makes woods available free of charge. Free coaching is available for beginners and for anyone wanting to refine their skills, bookable through the club. The club is also open most days and will arrange an alternative time for anyone who cannot make Tuesdays.
