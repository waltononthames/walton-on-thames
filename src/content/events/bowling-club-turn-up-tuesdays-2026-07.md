Walton-on-Thames Bowling Club invites members and guests to a casual roll-up game every Tuesday from 5pm, with light refreshments provided. The club says there is no need to book: "just turn up, say hello, and enjoy the game."

Newcomers do not need their own equipment, as the club makes woods available free of charge. The club was founded in 1922 and plays on the green at Elm Grove Recreation Ground.

The outdoor season runs from April to the middle of October, after which the roll-up continues indoors on short mat.

*Sources: [Walton-on-Thames Bowling Club](https://www.waltonbowlingclub.org.uk/) for the session and the club's own details; [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/whats-on/) for the 8pm finish, which the club's own page does not state. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
