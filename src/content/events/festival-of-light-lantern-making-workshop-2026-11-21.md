As part of Walton's Festival of Light, Imogen Andrews runs lantern making workshops for the community in the Riverhouse Barn studio.

There are three sessions on Saturday 21 November, starting at 1pm, 2pm and 3pm, each lasting about an hour. The £5 charge covers the materials for the lantern kit. Places are limited to 10 per session, so booking ahead is advised.
