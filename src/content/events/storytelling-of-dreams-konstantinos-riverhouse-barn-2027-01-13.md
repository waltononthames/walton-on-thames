Self-taught painter Konstantinos shows 30 paintings spanning 20 years in the Robert Phillips Gallery. Working in oil, he draws on Greece, India and England, where he grew up, lived and worked, in allegorical figurative paintings in the metaphysical tradition.

The exhibition is open daily from 10am to 4pm, from Wednesday 13 to Monday 18 January 2027, and admission is free.
