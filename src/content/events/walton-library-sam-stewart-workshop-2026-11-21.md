Sam Stewart, author of the Fireblood fantasy series, runs an interactive workshop where children create maps, invent beasts and develop characters and story ideas of their own. It finishes with questions and a chance to have books signed. The event is part of Walton Library's National Year of Reading programme and is suitable for children aged 8 to 14.

Tickets are £3 per child, bookable online or at the library counter.
