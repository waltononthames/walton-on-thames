Riverhouse Barn hosts a commemorative fundraising concert that reimagines the original 1986 Quartet Amongst the Puddles, the event that helped raise the funds to transform an 18th century barn into the arts centre it is today.

Performed by Quartet Concrète. The programme is to be announced by the venue. The concert runs from 3pm to 4pm.
