Learn the basic traditional skills of silver jewellery making and complete your own design. Under Sarah's guidance each student has the opportunity to design and create a unique piece of jewellery.

Classes run on Mondays from 10am to 12noon or 4pm to 6pm, and on Fridays from 10am to 12noon. Tools are provided, but materials are extra. To enrol, email sarahlevy37@yahoo.com.
