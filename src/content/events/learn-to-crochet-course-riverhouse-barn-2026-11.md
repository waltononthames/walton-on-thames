Mezzacraft is running a second autumn run of its beginners' crochet course at Riverhouse Barn, on Tuesday evenings from 7:30pm to 9:30pm on 3, 10, 17 and 24 November 2026. The organiser says the first week is compulsory.

The course suits complete beginners, people who have dabbled without getting to grips with it, anyone returning after many years, and self-taught crocheters who want to check their technique. Classes take a maximum of eight students, and the £98 fee includes a kit with hook, yarn, needle, stitch markers and a project bag, plus printed class notes.
