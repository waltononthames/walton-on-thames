Walton's farmers' market is a popular fixture in the town's social calendar, run by Surrey Markets on the first Saturday of every month, right outside The Heart Shopping Centre.

Expect a good variety of seasonal vegetables and fruit, freshly baked bread and pastries, local honey, cheeses, free-range meat and eggs, and speciality preserves. A great opportunity to stock up on quality local food and have a chat with the people who grew or made it.

**Practical information:**
- First Saturday of every month, 9:30am to 2pm
- Free entry
- Parking available at the multi-storey car park next to Sainsbury's
- Nearest station: Walton-on-Thames (short walk)
