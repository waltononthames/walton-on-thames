A five week daytime course making a simple, relaxed fit, short-sleeved cardigan in DK yarn from a pattern. There is no calculating or designing involved: choose a size, match the pattern gauge and start crocheting.

The venue describes it as a good course for improving pattern reading skills, or for anyone who wants a longer, more relaxed course in which to tackle a larger project with support.

The five sessions run on Mondays from 12:30pm to 2:30pm: 14, 21 and 28 September, then 5 and 12 October.
