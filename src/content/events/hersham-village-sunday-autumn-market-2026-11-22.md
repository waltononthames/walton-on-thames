Hersham Village Market adds a Sunday afternoon autumn market to its usual Friday mornings, at All Saints Church Hall on Queens Road.

The listing promises the market's regular mix of home-cooked food, local honey and preserves, free-range eggs, artisan crafts and cards, and seasonal plants and vegetables, with a cafe for tea, coffee and cake. Admission and parking are free.

*Source: [Your Elmbridge Magazine](https://yourelmbridge.co.uk/whats-on-in-elmbridge/), which gives the 1:30pm to 4pm times; the organiser's own [Hersham Village Market page](https://www.facebook.com/hershamvillagemarket) had not yet posted this date when checked. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
