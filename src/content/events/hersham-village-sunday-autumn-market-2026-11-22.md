Hersham Village Market holds a Sunday afternoon Christmas market at All Saints Catholic Church Hall on Queens Road, from 1:30pm to 4pm, in place of its usual Friday morning. There is no market on Friday 20 November.

The market's flyer promises home-cooked food, seasonal plants and vegetables, preserves, local honey, free-range eggs, gifts and cards, with Bob's Cafe for refreshments. Entry and parking are free, and card payments are welcomed.
