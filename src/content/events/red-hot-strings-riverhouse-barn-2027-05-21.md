After three sold-out years at Riverhouse Barn, violin duo Red Hot Strings return with a mix of classical favourites, pop and rock anthems, dance tracks and songs from their debut album Crystal, played on classical and LED electric violins. Special guests join them on stage.

Tickets are £20 and the evening runs from 7:30pm to 10pm.
