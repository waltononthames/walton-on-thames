Photographer Constantine shows street photographs from 14 years of walking the streets and markets of Mumbai. Rather than the tourist sights, the pictures look for the everyday life of the city, its hard work, kindness and contradictions.

The exhibition is open daily from 10am to 4pm, from Wednesday 6 to Monday 11 January 2027, and admission is free.
