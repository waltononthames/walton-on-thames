St Peter's Active Men's group, known as SPAM, meets on the second Monday of each month at The Royal George on Hersham Road. There is no set agenda: people arrive at any time from 8pm onwards and leave when they want.

The church describes it as an informal gathering and a good opportunity to get to know others involved with St Peter's. Tony Skrzypecki coordinates the group, and the parish office can be reached on 01932 253452 or at theoffice@stpetershersham.com.
