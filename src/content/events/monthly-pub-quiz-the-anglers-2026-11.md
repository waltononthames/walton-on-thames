The Anglers, the riverside pub on Manor Road, holds a pub quiz on the second Wednesday of every month from 7pm. The next one is on Wednesday 11 November 2026, running until 10pm.

Rounds mix general knowledge, music and pictures. Entry is £2 per person, teams are welcome, and first prize is a £50 bar tab. The kitchen is open until 9pm. To register, email your details to hello@anglerswalton.co.uk.

*Source: [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/event/monthly-pub-quiz-at-the-anglers/). [The Anglers' own website](https://www.anglerswalton.co.uk/) does not list the quiz, so no organiser listing could be found. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
