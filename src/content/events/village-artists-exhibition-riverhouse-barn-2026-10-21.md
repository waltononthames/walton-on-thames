Village Artists is a group of local artists exhibiting together in the Robert Phillips Gallery, showing original artwork at what they describe as realistic, affordable prices.

The gallery will be stewarded mainly by one or more of the participating artists, who the group says will be happy to talk about their work. The venue notes that owning an original piece means being the only person in the world who owns that particular piece.

The exhibition runs daily from 10am to 4pm, from Wednesday 21 October to Sunday 8 November 2026, with free admission.
