Mezzacraft's Merrian Holland teaches a four week beginners' crochet course in the studio at Riverhouse Barn, for complete beginners and for anyone returning to crochet or wanting to check their self-taught technique. Over the four sessions students cover the basic stitches, reading chart symbols, making circles and squares, changing colours and joining motifs, finishing with a small completed project. Classes take up to eight students.

Two groups start in January 2027:

- **Tuesday evenings**, 7:30pm to 9:30pm on 12, 19 and 26 January and 2 February.
- **Saturday afternoons**, 3pm to 5pm on 16, 23 and 30 January and 6 February.

Each course costs £100, which includes a kit with a hook, yarn and other materials, printed class notes and video tutorials. The first week is compulsory. Booking is through Mezzacraft.
