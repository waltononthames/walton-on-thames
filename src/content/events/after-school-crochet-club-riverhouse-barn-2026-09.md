Crochet artist and tutor Susan Blandford (MA Textiles) runs an after-school crochet club for ages 10 and over on Mondays from 4pm to 5:30pm.

Participants learn the essential crochet stitches while completing projects including friendship bracelets, crochet flowers to decorate cards, granny squares and a crochet pairs game. No previous experience is needed.
