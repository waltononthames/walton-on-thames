Life drawing runs every Monday from 6:30pm to 8:30pm at Riverhouse Barn, with a professional model posing each week and support, instruction and critique offered by internationally trained artist Stephen Jones.

Booking ahead is advised by calling Stephen on 07565 908092, though walk-ins are accepted subject to space. Bring your own paper (A1 maximum) and drawing board.
