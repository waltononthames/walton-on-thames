Intergenerational Music Making runs two Read to the Beat workshops at Walton Library on Tuesday 10 November, starting at 10.30am and 1.30pm, both suitable for under-5s. Families explore a featured book, its rhythms and sounds, then create and perform an original song inspired by it.

Tickets are available with a suggested donation of £2 per person and must be booked in advance.
