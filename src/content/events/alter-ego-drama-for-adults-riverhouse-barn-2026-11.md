Alter Ego runs workshop-style drama sessions for adults at Riverhouse Barn on Tuesdays from 8pm to 9:30pm. Drama games and acting exercises build performance skills without the pressure of putting on a production.

No experience is needed, and new members can join at any point in the term after a free taster session. The cost depends on how many weeks are left in the term. To book, contact Caroline Dooley at enquiries@drama4adults.com or see www.drama4adults.com.
