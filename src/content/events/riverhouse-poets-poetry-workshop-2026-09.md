Whether you have experience writing poems or are simply curious to see what happens when your pen meets paper, this workshop uses lively prompts, stimulating suggestions and fun exercises to help you unleash your inner poet.

Workshops run fortnightly on Thursdays from 1:30pm to 3:45pm, with autumn dates on 24 September, 8 and 22 October, 5 and 19 November and 3 December 2026. The last session, on 3 December, is a feedback session. Each workshop also stands alone: the venue sells drop-in places at £10 a session, or £50 for the term, and recommends booking.
