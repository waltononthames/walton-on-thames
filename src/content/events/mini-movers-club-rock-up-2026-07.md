Rock Up runs its Mini Movers Club for babies and toddlers every Thursday at its indoor climbing centre in The Heart Shopping Centre, describing it as "Baby & Toddler sessions every Thursday, unlimited hot drinks & mountains of fun!"

The toddler sessions combine climbing in a colourful arena with soft play, and are aimed at under-fives. Booking is through the centre.
