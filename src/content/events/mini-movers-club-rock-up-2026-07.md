Rock Up runs its Mini Movers Club, a weekly baby and toddler session, on Thursday mornings at its indoor climbing centre in The Heart Shopping Centre. Love Walton lists the sessions from 9am to 12pm on 19 and 26 November and 3 December.

The Heart announced the club's launch in July 2026 as weekly baby and toddler soft play sessions hosted by Rock Up. Booking is through the centre.

*Sources: [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/whats-on/) for the dates and the 9am to 12pm timing; [The Heart Shopping Centre](https://heartshopping.co.uk/whats-on/2026/07/01/new-weekly-baby-and-toddler-mornings-launch-this-thursday-at-rock-up!/) for the club's launch as a weekly Thursday session. Rock Up's own [Walton-on-Thames page](https://www.rock-up.co.uk/locations/walton-on-thames) no longer mentions the club, so no current organiser listing could be found.
