Harmony's Music Club returns to Riverhouse Barn with a Christmas concert built around a live performance of The Snowman, which the venue says is "back by popular demand".

The story is narrated by children's narrator Alexander Campbell, accompanied by violin, cello and piano. The second half fills the barn with Christmas music including In the Bleak Midwinter. There are two performances on Sunday 20 December 2026, from 11am to 12:30pm and from 2pm to 3:30pm. Tickets are £16 for adults and £12 for children aged 2 to 12, and under-2s go free. When checked on 23 November 2026, the venue showed the 2pm performance as sold out.

*Sources: [Riverhouse Barn](https://www.riverhousebarn.co.uk/events/184077) for the morning performance and [the venue's listing for the afternoon performance](https://www.riverhousebarn.co.uk/events/179714). Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
