Harmony's Music Club returns to Riverhouse Barn with a Christmas concert built around a live performance of The Snowman, which the venue says is "back by popular demand".

The story is narrated by children's narrator Alexander Campbell, accompanied by violin, cello and piano. The second half fills the barn with Christmas music including In the Bleak Midwinter. The venue lists tickets from free to £16.
