Walton Art Club returns to Riverhouse Barn with its twice-yearly showcase of new artwork. The club meets weekly to paint, draw and work together, and its members range from beginners to experienced painters and illustrators. Work is on display and for sale.

The exhibition is open daily from 10am to 4pm, Wednesday 11 to Sunday 15 November 2026. Admission is free and all are welcome.
