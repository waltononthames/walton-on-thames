A sound bath uses vibrational sound to encourage relaxation. Participants are invited to lie down, close their eyes and listen to Himalayan and crystal singing bowls and percussive sound.

Sessions run from 6:30pm to 7:30pm in the Robert Phillips Gallery, with autumn term dates on 6 October, 3 and 17 November and 1 December 2026.

Booking is direct with the organiser rather than through the Riverhouse box office, by email to hummingbirdhandw@gmail.com or by calling or messaging Claire on 07930 461441. Riverhouse Barn does not publish a ticket price for these sessions.
