Composer, guitarist and academic Milton Mermikides traces the relationship between musicians and technology, from early musical automata and the first recordings of the human voice to electronic instruments and artificial intelligence, with stories, demonstrations and musical examples.

The Royston Pike Lectures are presented by Elmbridge Borough Council with Riverhouse Barn. The lecture runs from 7:30pm to 9pm on Wednesday 13 January 2027, and tickets are £5.
