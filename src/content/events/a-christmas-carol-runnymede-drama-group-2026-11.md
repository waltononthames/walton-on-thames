Runnymede Drama Group brings a new version of Dickens' ghost story to Cecil Hepworth Playhouse. The group says it stays true to the original tale while adding humour, song, puppetry and dance, with familiar Christmas carols given new lyrics.

Performances run Wednesday 11 to Friday 13 November 2026 at 7:30pm, with two performances on Saturday 14 November at 2:30pm and 7:30pm. Tickets for the Wednesday performance were listed at £16.17 including the booking fee.
