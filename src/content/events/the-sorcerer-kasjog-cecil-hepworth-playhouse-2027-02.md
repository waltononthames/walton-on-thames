KASJOG bring Gilbert and Sullivan's The Sorcerer to Cecil Hepworth Playhouse from Wednesday 24 to Saturday 27 February 2027. One of the pair's earliest works, it follows a well-meaning young aristocrat who engages a magician to dose an entire village with a love potion, and the trouble that follows.

Performances are at 7:30pm from Wednesday to Friday, and at 2pm and 7pm on the Saturday. Ellie Parsons directs, with Brian Steel as musical director. The company says tickets go on sale on 1 December 2026; no prices have been published yet, and the playhouse gives 0333 666 3366 as the ticket line.

*Sources: [KASJOG](https://www.kasjog.co.uk/) for the dates, creative team and on-sale date, and [Cecil Hepworth Playhouse](https://www.cecilhepworthplayhouse.co.uk/whats-on) for the performance times and ticket line. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
