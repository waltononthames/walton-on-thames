As part of its National Year of Reading events in November, Walton Library hosts author Diane M. Hinds talking about her novel Conquerabia: The Struggle for Identity, which traces the history of Trinidad. She will discuss how historical research becomes fiction and the challenges of bringing forgotten voices to life, with time for questions.

Tickets are £3 per person, bookable online or at the library counter.
