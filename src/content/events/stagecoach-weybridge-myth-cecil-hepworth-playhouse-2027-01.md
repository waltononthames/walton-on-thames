Stagecoach Weybridge brings Myth, described by the company as a Greek-inspired musical, to Cecil Hepworth Playhouse for a run of nine performances between Tuesday 26 and Sunday 31 January 2027. Each performance is given by a different one of the company's schools.

The advertised times are Tuesday 26 January at 7pm, Wednesday 27 January at 6pm, Thursday 28 January at 7pm and Friday 29 January at 6pm, all running for an hour and a half. Saturday 30 January has performances at 11am, 1:45pm and 7pm, and Sunday 31 January at 11am and 1:45pm.

Tickets are £11 for children under 11 and £15 for adults and children aged 11 and over, which TicketSource shows as £11.92 to £16.26 once the booking fee is added. Booking opens on Thursday 1 October 2026 at 10am. The company notes that tickets are non-refundable and specific to a particular school's performance, and that everyone in the audience needs a ticket apart from babies in arms.
