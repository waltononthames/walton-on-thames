MMT and MDA bring their Christmas pantomime Once Upon a Time in Pantoland to Cecil Hepworth Playhouse from 17 to 20 December, billed as "Magic, Mayhem & Laughs Galore".

Performances start at 7:30pm on Thursday 17 and Friday 18 December, 1:30pm on Saturday 19 December and 2:30pm on Sunday 20 December. The closing time above is estimated, as an exact finish time was not published.

Tickets are sold through TicketSource. Children's tickets are valid for ages 3 to 12, and group bookings of ten or more go through the box office on 020 8941 3255.
