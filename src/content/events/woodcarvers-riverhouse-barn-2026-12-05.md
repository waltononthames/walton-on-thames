The Riverhouse Barn woodcarvers are a friendly, informal group with no tutor: members help each other with whatever project they choose. The last session of the autumn term is on Saturday 5 December 2026, from 10am to 12:30pm in the Toshiba Studio. The group meets fortnightly in term time.

Sessions cost £8. Some wood is available, but bringing your own is better, and tools are provided though you are welcome to bring your own.
