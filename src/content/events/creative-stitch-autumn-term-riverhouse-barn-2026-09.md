Creative Stitch is a relaxed and friendly ten week course run by qualified embroiderer Janet McDonagh, combining techniques such as batik, felting and machine sewing with more traditional hand embroidery.

Three slots run each week: Wednesday mornings 10am to 12pm, Wednesday afternoons 12:30pm to 2:30pm, and Thursday mornings 10:30am to 12:30pm. The autumn term starts on Wednesday 23 September 2026 (Thursday 24 September for the Thursday class) and runs for ten weeks with one week off at half term. On the first day students only need a pen or pencil to make notes.
