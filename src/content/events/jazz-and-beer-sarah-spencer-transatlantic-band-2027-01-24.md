Sarah Spencer started the Transatlantic Band in 1997 while she was based in the United States, and the group now makes a yearly return to Riverhouse Barn. Their music follows New Orleans from its 1920s dance halls, through the New Orleans Revival, to the rhythm and blues and rock and roll of performers such as Fats Domino and Little Richard.

Tickets are £15 and the Sunday lunchtime session runs from 12:15pm to 2:15pm.
