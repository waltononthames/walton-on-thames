Life drawing runs every Monday from 6:30pm to 8:30pm at Riverhouse Barn, with a professional model posing each week. Artist Stephen Jones offers support, instruction and critique, and the class is open to all levels.

Book ahead by calling Stephen on 07565 908092; walk-ins are accepted if there is space. Bring your own paper (A1 at most), a drawing board, charcoal, chalk, pencils or pens and an eraser, and an easel if you can. Paint and liquid ink are not allowed.
