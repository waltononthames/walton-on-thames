Tutor Sarah Levy teaches the basic traditional skills of silver jewellery making, with each student designing and making a piece of their own.

Classes run on Mondays from 10am to 12noon or 4pm to 6pm, and on Fridays from 10am to 12noon. Tools are provided, but materials cost extra. To enrol, email sarahlevy37@yahoo.com.
