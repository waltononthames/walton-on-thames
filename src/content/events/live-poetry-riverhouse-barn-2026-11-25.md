Riverhouse Barn's monthly spoken word series returns with guest poets of national standing reading and discussing their work, plus the open mic spots for anyone who wants to read their own. The evening is hosted by Riverhouse Poets tutor Katie Griffiths and runs from 7:30pm to 9:30pm.

This month Katie Griffiths is in conversation with headliners Jacqueline Saphra, a poet, playwright and librettist whose collection All My Mad Mothers was shortlisted for the TS Eliot Prize, and John McCullough, whose Reckless Paper Birds won the 2020 Hawthornden Prize for Literature. Tickets are £5.
