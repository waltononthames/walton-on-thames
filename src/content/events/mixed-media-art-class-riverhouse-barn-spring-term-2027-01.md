Janet McDonagh's mixed media art class runs at Riverhouse Barn on Friday afternoons from 1pm to 3pm for ten weeks of the spring term, starting on 15 January 2027. It is open to all levels of experience.

Students try out a range of materials, including watercolour, pastels, acrylics, alcohol inks, oils, pencil and charcoal, working partly from observation and partly from other artists' styles. A course plan is handed out at the start of term. Only basic materials are provided; Janet brings paints to try and paper to buy. The course costs £120.
