Pianist Richard Uttley, violinist Nathaniel Anderson-Frank and cellist Ella Rundle perform together as the Wren Piano Trio. The programme is Turina's Circulo, Tailleferre's Piano Trio and Mendelssohn's Piano Trio No. 2 in C minor.

Tickets are £16 and the concert runs from 1pm to 2pm.
