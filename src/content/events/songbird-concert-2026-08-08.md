Irish soprano Eve Stafford performs her UK debut at St Peter's Church in Hersham, accompanied on piano by her cousin Robert Stafford in their first musical collaboration since discovering their family connection through genealogy research. The programme blends classical favourites and musical theatre anthems, including Danny Boy, Nella Fantasia and songs from The Sound of Music. Doors open at 7.30pm with open seating; admission is free, with a retiring collection towards the church organ restoration fund. Registration is requested via bookings@evestafford.ie.
