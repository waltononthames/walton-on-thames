Our ArtClub runs weekly on Thursdays from 4:45pm to 5:45pm for children in school years 1 to 6. Students work with a variety of techniques and formats, building a personal portfolio of their work over the term. The first part of the autumn term ran from 17 September to 15 October, and the venue lists the next session on Thursday 12 November.

Spaces are limited and places are booked by emailing ourartclub4fun@gmail.com.
