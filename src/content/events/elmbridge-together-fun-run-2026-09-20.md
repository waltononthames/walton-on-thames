The Elmbridge Together Fun Run is hosted by the Mayor of Elmbridge, Councillor Judy Sarsby, at the Elmbridge Xcel Sports Hub. A children's 2km event starts at 2.30pm and a 5km riverside run, jog or walk starts at 3pm.

Profits go to the Mayor's chosen charities, Elmbridge CAN and the Mayor of Elmbridge Trust Fund. Registration is open through the Enthuse platform.

*Sources: [Elmbridge Borough Council](https://www.elmbridge.gov.uk/news/2026/elmbridge-mayor-launches-elmbridge-together-fun-run-support-local-charities) and [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/whats-on/). Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
