The Saturday Night Project returns for summer 2026, giving 11 to 15 year olds free access to sports pitches, inflatables, pool inflatables and general swimming at Xcel Leisure Complex. Sessions run 6pm to 8pm on 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29 August, with no booking required.
