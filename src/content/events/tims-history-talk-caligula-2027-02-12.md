Tim's latest history talk at Riverhouse Barn turns to Caligula, the Roman emperor whose name has become shorthand for a tyrant. It weighs what can actually be known about him against the stories told since, including the claim that he made his horse a consul, and asks whether there was more to him than madness.

The talk runs from 7pm to 9pm on Friday 12 February 2027 and tickets are £10.
