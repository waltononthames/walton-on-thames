St Peter's Church runs a Community Cafe every Wednesday between 10am and 12pm, open to everybody in the local community. It offers coffee, cake and friendly conversation in a comfortable setting.

The church describes the cost as pay what you can, if you can. The cafe runs on the same day as the Walton and Hersham Foodbank.
