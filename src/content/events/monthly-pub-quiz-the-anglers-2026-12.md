The Anglers, the riverside pub on Manor Road, holds a pub quiz on the second Wednesday of every month from 7pm. The next one is on Wednesday 9 December 2026, running until 10pm.

Rounds mix general knowledge, music and pictures, with a few curveballs. Entry is £2 per person and teams are welcome. First prize is a £50 bar tab and second prize is bottles of house wine. The kitchen is open until 9pm. To register, email your details to hello@anglerswalton.co.uk.

*Source: [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/whats-on/), which lists the 9 December quiz. [The Anglers' own website](https://www.anglerswalton.co.uk/) does not list the quiz, so no organiser listing could be found. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
