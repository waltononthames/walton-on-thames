Nick Pollard, curator of Spelthorne Museum, chairman of the Sunbury and Shepperton Local History Society and author of The History of Walton Bridge, gives a talk on the six bridges that have spanned the Thames between Walton and Shepperton.

Places are limited and booked at the Walton Library counter.
