Virtuoso guitarists Ben Bruant and Will Cashel, who met at the Royal Academy of Music, return to Riverhouse Barn as Duo Destino with a blend of flamenco, classical, Latin and contemporary music, from Albéniz and Piazzolla to their own compositions.

There are two shows, on Friday 7 May and Saturday 8 May 2027, each starting at 8pm and lasting two hours including a 20-minute interval. Tickets are £15 to £19.
