The Riverhouse woodcarvers are a friendly, informal group who meet in the Toshiba Studio from 10am to 12:30pm. There is no tutor, so members help each other and everyone chips in.

You can attempt any project you wish, limited only by the tools available, the size of the wood and your imagination. Some wood is available, but bringing your own is preferred. Lime carves well, though an old post can work just as well.
