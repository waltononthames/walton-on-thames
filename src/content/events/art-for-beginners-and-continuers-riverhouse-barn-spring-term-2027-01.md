Janet McDonagh's ten week art course for adults returns to Riverhouse Barn for the spring term, on Thursday evenings from 6:15pm to 7:45pm, starting on 14 January 2027. It is open to all levels of experience.

The course moves between watercolour, pastels, acrylics, pencil and charcoal. Some sessions draw from observation and others look at the styles of other artists. Only basic materials are provided: pencils, charcoal and similar items can be used from the art room, and Janet brings paints to try and paper to buy. The course costs £120.
