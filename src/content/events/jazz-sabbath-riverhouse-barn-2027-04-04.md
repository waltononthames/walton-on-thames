Jazz Sabbath are back at Riverhouse Barn. The trio is led by pianist Adam Wakeman, who has played keys for Black Sabbath and Ozzy Osbourne since 2004, and plays new jazz arrangements of Black Sabbath songs.

The concert runs from 7:30pm to 9:30pm and tickets are £32.50.
