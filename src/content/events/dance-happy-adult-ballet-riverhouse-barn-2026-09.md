Jo runs two adult ballet classes at Riverhouse Barn on Tuesdays during term time, at 5:30pm to 6:30pm and 6:40pm to 7:40pm. Each class begins with a barre workout, and while some technique is covered the main focus is on the joy of movement and musicality.
