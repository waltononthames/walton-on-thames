Love Walton is working with Elmbridge Beat (Surrey Police) and Crimestoppers on a roadshow at The Heart Shopping Centre, as part of the National Business Crime Centre's Safer Business Action Week.

The team will promote the importance of being kind to shop workers, explain how to report crime and anti-social behaviour to the police, and offer reminders on keeping yourself and your home safe as the darker winter evenings and the festive season arrive. They will be in The Heart Plaza, weather permitting, from 10:30am to 3pm on Tuesday 10 November.
