The Magical Mr West, the stage name of performer Rob Tay, and his corvid companion Crowbert explore the science of illusions, with optical illusions, tricks to try at home and a look at the deceptions used to sell us ideas.

The show is suitable for ages 6 and up and lasts 60 minutes with no interval. Young siblings under 2 who do not take a seat go free.
