Walton Folk's January date brings Gilmore & Roberts to Riverhouse Barn. Katriona Gilmore (fiddle, mandolin) and Jamie Roberts (guitar) met at Leeds College of Music, have been nominated three times at the BBC Radio 2 Folk Awards, and are known for their songwriting and harmonies. The duo marked 20 years together in 2026.

Tickets are £16 and the lunchtime concert runs from 12:15pm to 2:15pm.
