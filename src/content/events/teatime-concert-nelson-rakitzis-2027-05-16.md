Violinist Oliver Nelson, a Royal Academy of Music graduate whose playing features in Francois Girard's film The Song of Names, joins pianist Vasilis Rakitzis for a teatime concert at Riverhouse Barn.

The programme includes Richard Strauss's Sonata for Violin and Piano in E flat, Op. 18. The concert runs from 5pm to 6pm and tickets are £16.
