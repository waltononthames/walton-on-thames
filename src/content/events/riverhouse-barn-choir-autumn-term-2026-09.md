The Riverhouse Barn Choir returns for its autumn term under musical director Johnny Kilhams, starting on Tuesday 15 September 2026 with rehearsals from 10am to 11:30am.

Ten sessions run across the term: 15, 22 and 29 September, 6 and 13 October, 3, 10, 17 and 24 November and 1 December. The 22 September and 24 November rehearsals are shorter, running 10am to 11am, and the drop-in day ticket is not available on those two dates. Cash is welcome on the door.
