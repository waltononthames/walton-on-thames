The December talk, "The Christmas Schedule: A Celebration of Great British Festive TV", looks back at the golden age of Christmas viewing on British television.

The afternoon screening is Mary Poppins (1964, rated PG), starring Julie Andrews. The Trust says it launches a series of Julie Andrews films to be shown in 2027 as part of its Silver Screen programme.

The talk begins at 11:30am and the film at 1:30pm. Tickets are £5 for the talk or the screening and £8 for both, with cash payment at the door.
