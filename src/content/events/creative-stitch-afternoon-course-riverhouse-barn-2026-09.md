Janet McDonagh's afternoon Creative Stitch course runs on Wednesdays from 12:30pm to 2:30pm, alongside the existing morning course. It is a relaxed ten week class combining techniques such as batik, felting and machine sewing with more traditional hand embroidery.

Students need only a pen or pencil for the first session, which is mostly a discussion of how to approach the project.
