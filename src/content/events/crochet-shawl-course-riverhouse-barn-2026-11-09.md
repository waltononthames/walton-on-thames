Mezzacraft is running a four week crochet shawl course at Riverhouse Barn on Monday afternoons from 12:30pm to 2:30pm, on 9, 16, 23 and 30 November 2026. An evening version runs on the same Mondays at St Erconwald's Church Hall.

Students make a top down triangle shawl with textured stitches, in a large or smaller bandana size, following a pattern designed for the optional "assigned pooling" technique, which needs specially dyed yarn. The course is for crocheters who are already confident with basic stitches and counting stitches. The £80 fee covers tuition, printed class notes and pattern, and video tutorials; tools and materials are not included.
