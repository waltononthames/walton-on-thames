Fabric Alchemy is the annual exhibition of Wey Valley Textile Arts, a long-established group working in textiles and mixed media, formerly known as Wey Valley Workshop.

The group says stewards will be on hand each day to answer questions, and that the work on show can be viewed and bought.

The exhibition runs daily from 10am to 4pm, from Wednesday 7 to Sunday 18 October 2026, with free admission.
