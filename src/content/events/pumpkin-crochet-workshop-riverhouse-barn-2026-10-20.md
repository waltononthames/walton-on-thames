A half-term crochet workshop for 10 to 18 year olds, making a Halloween and autumn bunting on a pumpkin theme. Beginners are welcome and materials and the pattern are provided, with a £5 donation asked to cover the cost of materials.

The workshop is taught by crochet artist and tutor Susan Blandford.
