Andy Mais, who has already given two charity concerts at Riverhouse Barn, returns on Saturday 16 January 2027 for an informal evening of piano music and songs, from 7pm to 9pm.

The programme mixes classical pieces by Beethoven and Rachmaninov with contemporary music ranging from Ed Sheeran to Tim Minchin, plus some jazz, improvisation and Andy's own compositions. Andy is a classically trained amateur pianist who has more recently taken up jazz and singing.

All proceeds go to two charities, ANDYSMANCLUB and The Amber Trust. Tickets are £16 to attend in person. The concert is also livestreamed on the venue's YouTube channel for £12, with the link sent out in the week before.
