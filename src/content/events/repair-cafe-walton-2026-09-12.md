Bring broken household items, clothing, small electricals and more along to the Repair Cafe, where volunteer fixers offer free hands-on help to get things working again.

The September session moves to St Erconwald's Church on Esher Avenue rather than the Repair Cafe's usual home at Walton Centre for the Community. Your Elmbridge describes the venue as temporarily relocated, so check before travelling.
