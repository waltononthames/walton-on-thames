Qualified embroiderer Janet McDonagh runs three ten week creative stitch and textile classes at Riverhouse Barn in the spring term of 2027:

- Wednesday mornings, 10am to 12pm, from 13 January
- Wednesday afternoons, 12:30pm to 2:30pm, from 13 January
- Thursday mornings, 10:30am to 12:30pm, from 14 January, in the Toshiba Craft Studio

Each term opens with a workshop session introducing new techniques for the term's projects. The classes cover hand and machine stitching, with an emphasis on making backgrounds to stitch on and on reusing donated fabrics and threads. Students are encouraged to keep a stitch diary or sampler. Work from the course goes on show in the Robert Phillips Gallery exhibition from 30 June to 11 July 2027. Each class costs £115 for the ten weeks.
