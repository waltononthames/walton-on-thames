The Elmbridge Community Ecohub is hosting a toy swap on Saturday 28 November, with doors open from 9:30am until 12pm. Families can bring toys, games, puzzles and Christmas outfits for children aged 0 to 7 that they no longer need, and exchange tokens for new-to-you items. Books are not accepted, though the ecohub runs its own book swap.

Toys can be dropped off in advance on Friday 27 November between 8pm and 9pm or on the morning of the swap between 8:45am and 9:30am, or brought along during the swap itself, when a maximum of five items is accepted. Items must be clean, in good working order, CE marked and complete, and battery-powered toys should come charged or with batteries. Anyone with nothing to swap is asked for a small donation to the ecohub. Leftover toys go to the ecohub or a local charity.

The event is part of The Big Toy Swap, a National Lottery funded campaign run by No Crap Parties. The listing advises setting maps to Rydens Grove rather than the ecohub itself, as the route Google Maps suggests through Three Rivers School has no access.

*Source: [Love Walton](https://lovewalton.co.uk/event/the-big-elmbridge-toyswap/). Registration is through a Facebook event, and no organiser listing outside Facebook could be found. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
