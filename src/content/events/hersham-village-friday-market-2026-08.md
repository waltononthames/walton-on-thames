Hersham Village Market holds a Friday morning market at All Saints Church Hall on Queens Road, from 10.30am to 12pm. Stalls offer home-cooked food, local honey and preserves, free-range eggs, artisan crafts and cards, and seasonal plants and vegetables, and there is a cafe for tea, coffee and cake. Admission and parking are free.

The November markets are on 6, 13 and 27 November. No Friday market is listed for 20 November.

*Source: [Your Elmbridge Magazine](https://yourelmbridge.co.uk/whats-on-in-elmbridge/), which gives the November dates and the times; the organiser's own [Hersham Village Market page](https://www.facebook.com/hershamvillagemarket) is on Facebook and could not be read when checked. Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
