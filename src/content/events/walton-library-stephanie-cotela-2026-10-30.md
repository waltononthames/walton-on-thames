Local writer Stephanie Cotela, author of the spooky stories The Ghosts of Hawkthorn and Gossamer House, hosts an afternoon of interactive storytelling, games and Halloween-themed fun at Walton Library.

The event is free, but places are limited and must be booked at the library counter.
