Walton Library's Digital Support Volunteer offers free half-hour appointments to help with computer, device and internet queries, every Tuesday from 5:45pm to 6:45pm.

Appointments are recommended and can be booked at the library helpdesk.

*Held every Tuesday. Source: [Surrey County Council](https://www.surreycc.gov.uk/libraries/your-library/find-your-nearest/walton/events-and-activities). Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
