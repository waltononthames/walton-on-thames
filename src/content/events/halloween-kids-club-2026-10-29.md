Love Walton runs three free 45-minute Halloween-themed Kids Club sessions on Walton High Street over the October half term, each pairing a story time with a Halloween craft. The sessions are run in collaboration with Helen Hamill, illustrator of The Buttonhole Press.

The three sessions on Thursday 29 October run 10.15am to 11am, 11am to 11.45am and 11.45am to 12.30pm. Places are free but limited and must be booked in advance, and a parent or guardian must stay for the session.
