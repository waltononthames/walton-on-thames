Walton Voices welcomes back Joanna Tomlinson for a second vocal technique workshop, following her previous one with the choir. The choir describes her as a conductor, singer and vocal coach, Artistic Director of the Military Wives Choirs, and conductor of London's Constanza Chorus and Whitehall Choir.

The three-hour practical workshop covers healthy vocal technique, building confidence and getting the best from your voice, putting the skills into practice with selected movements from Brahms' Requiem, sung in English.
