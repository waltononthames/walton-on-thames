Hersham Sports & Social Club hosts a Live Music Club every Tuesday evening, with a changing line-up of performers. Doors open from 7pm and acts start from 8pm. Entry is free and everyone is welcome, including non-members.

*Held every Tuesday. Sources: [Hersham Sports & Social Club](https://www.hershamsportsandsocialclub.co.uk/tuesday-live-music-nights.html) and [Your Elmbridge Magazine](https://yourelmbridge.co.uk/whats-on-in-elmbridge/). Always verify details directly with the organiser before attending.*
