## What Walton Charity does today

Walton Charity is a registered charity (number 1185959) serving the area of the ancient parish of Walton-on-Thames and, beyond that, the wider Borough of Elmbridge. Its registered purposes include providing social housing in the form of almshouses, helping individuals in need, providing grant support and accommodation to local organisations, and managing allotments. In practice, this means the charity provides almshouse accommodation, self-contained flats with modern amenities and on-site support, for people aged over 55 on modest incomes who want to live independently within a supportive community.
