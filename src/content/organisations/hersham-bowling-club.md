Hersham Bowling Club has been playing at Coronation Playing Fields since 1900, and is one of the few local clubs that keeps going right through the winter by moving indoors to short mat bowls.

## About

The club dates from 1900 and plays at Coronation Playing Fields on Molesey Road. It is a registered charity, number 220949.

## Who it is for

Adults and older people, at any level of experience. The club states that new members of all abilities are welcome, whether they have played before or not, and that coaching and equipment are both available.

## Activities

Outdoor bowls through the summer, short mat bowls indoors through the winter, and social events across the whole year. The club publishes a fixture list for the current season on its own website.

## When and where

Play is at Coronation Playing Fields, Molesey Road. The club asks anyone thinking of visiting to make contact first rather than simply turning up, so that someone is there to meet them.

## Joining or taking part

Contact the club before visiting. The secretary, Malcolm Connelly, can be reached on 01932 240892, the social coordinator, Moira Brock, on 07712 676768, and the club by email at info@hershambowlingclub.co.uk.
