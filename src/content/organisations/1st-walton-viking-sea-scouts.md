The Viking Sea Scouts are the water-based scout group in Walton, running powerboating and river activities from a headquarters on Felix Road alongside everything a land group does.

## About

The group is a Sea Scout group in the Walton & Weybridge District, based at its own Scout headquarters on Felix Road, close to the river.

## Who it is for

Children and young people from four to eighteen, across five sections: Squirrels for ages four to six, Beavers for six to eight, Cubs for eight to ten and a half, Scouts for ten and a half to fourteen, and Explorers for fourteen to eighteen. That makes it one of the few local groups taking children as young as four.

## Activities

Scouting with a water programme on top: the group's own recent news covers powerboating and river activities, and it takes an interest in Thames water quality.

## When and where

Meetings are at the Felix Road headquarters. The district publishes the section nights as Beavers on Wednesdays, Cubs on Tuesdays and Scouts on Fridays.

## Joining or taking part

The group runs a waiting list. Families join the application list through Online Scout Manager, linked from the group's own website, and the group has published policies covering how children move up between sections.

## Getting there

The headquarters is on Felix Road in Walton-on-Thames, near the river.
