Walton-on-Thames Lawn Tennis Club plays on eight courts at Tennis Close, two of them covered by a dome through the winter. Adult membership is paused at present, but the club continues to welcome new junior members.

## About

The club has eight courts: six artificial grass courts, recently resurfaced, and two Plexipave acrylic courts. Five carry LED floodlighting and can be played on until 10.30pm, and two sit under a dome for winter play. There is also a practice wall with a mini court, and a clubhouse with a bar, a lounge and table tennis.

## Who it is for

Adults and juniors, across a range of standards, with competitive league play, coaching and social tennis. Adult membership is paused until March 2027 because the club is at capacity. New junior members are still welcome.

## Activities

League tennis, coaching and social play. The junior programme includes LTA-accredited coaching through the summer, holiday camps during school breaks, and competitive play in LTA competitions. The club ran its club championships in September 2025.

## When and where

Play runs year-round at Tennis Close, with the domed courts and the floodlights carrying the club through the winter months. Court bookings are handled through the club's own online booking system.

## Joining or taking part

Junior membership enquiries and court bookings both go through the club's ClubSpark pages. Adults wishing to join should note the pause until March 2027.
