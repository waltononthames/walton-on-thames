4th Walton-on-Thames Scout Group meets at its own headquarters on Cottimore Lane and runs three sections covering ages six to fourteen.

## About

The group has its own headquarters on Cottimore Lane in Walton-on-Thames. Recent news from the group includes its first race night and the launch of a new website.

## Who it is for

Children and young people from six to fourteen, in three sections: Beaver Scouts for ages six to eight, Cubs for eight to ten and a half, and Scouts for ten and a half to fourteen.

## Activities

Scouting activities across the three sections.

## When and where

Section meetings are held at the group's Cottimore Lane headquarters, KT12 2BX. The district publishes the nights as Beavers on Mondays, Cubs on Tuesdays and Scouts on Fridays.

## Joining or taking part

Young people can apply through the Join Us section of the group's website, or by using its youth application form.
