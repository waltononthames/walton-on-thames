Elmbridge Community Link runs weekly activity sessions for adults with learning disabilities or autism, from board games and cooking to all-sports sessions, at community centres across Elmbridge.

## About

Elmbridge Community Link, known as ECL, is a registered charity, number 1035501. It runs its sessions from local community centres in Walton-on-Thames and the wider Elmbridge area.

## Who it is for

Adults with learning disabilities or autism, or both. Sessions start from age sixteen, and one of the sports sessions is aimed specifically at young autistic adults aged sixteen and over.

## Activities

Board games, all-sports sessions, multi-activity days, music workshops, arts and crafts, gardening, cooking and singing.

## Joining or taking part

Contact the coordinator, Pier, on 07981 450320 or at coordinator@eclwalton.org.uk to join a session. The same contact handles volunteering: the charity takes on volunteers to help across its weekly sessions.
