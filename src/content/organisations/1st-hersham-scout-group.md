1st Hersham Scout Group runs a weekly programme for over 100 young members from its own headquarters in the village, covering the full age range from six to eighteen.

## About

The group is part of the Walton & Weybridge District in Surrey and operates from its own headquarters in Hersham. It has over 100 youth members. It is currently running a maintenance appeal to upgrade the electrical systems at its headquarters.

## Who it is for

Children and young people from six to eighteen, in four sections: Beavers for ages six to eight, Cubs for eight to ten, Scouts for ten to fourteen and Explorers for fourteen to eighteen.

## Activities

A weekly programme of Scouting activities across the four sections.

## When and where

Meetings are weekly, at the group's headquarters in Hersham. The district publishes the section nights as Beavers on Mondays, Cubs on Wednesdays and Scouts on Thursdays.

## Joining or taking part

The group asks anyone interested in joining, or in volunteering, to get in touch through the contact page on its website. Beavers has a dedicated waiting list, which is also reached through the site.
