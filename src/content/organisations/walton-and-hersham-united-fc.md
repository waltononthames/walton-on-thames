Walton & Hersham United FC reports more than 40 teams and over 1,000 playing members, from under-6 to under-18, and is based at the Elmbridge Xcel Sports Hub.

## About

The club is the resident community club at the Elmbridge Xcel Sports Hub, the £18 million facility on Waterside Drive, chosen for that role by Elmbridge Borough Council. It is also the official junior club for [Walton & Hersham FC](/things-to-do/walton-and-hersham-fc/). It describes its approach as football for all ability levels.

## Who it is for

Children and young people from under-6 to under-18. Teams are organised across boys' competitive, boys' development, girls', mixed and women's recreational categories, so there is a route in whether a child wants competitive football or not.

## Activities

Football, across more than 40 teams. The breadth of the categories is the point: the club sets out to place players of different abilities and ambitions in a suitable team rather than turning them away.

## When and where

Training and matches take place at the Elmbridge Xcel Sports Hub on Waterside Drive. Age-group arrangements vary and are published by the club.

## Joining or taking part

Families are asked to complete a registration form on the club's website expressing interest in a free trial. The club then replies with available dates and where a child might fit.

## Getting there

The Elmbridge Xcel Sports Hub is on Waterside Drive, Walton-on-Thames KT12 2JP.
