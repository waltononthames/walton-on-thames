Walton & Hersham Youth FC plays at Elm Grove Recreation Ground and is now into its thirteenth season. It is a separate organisation from Walton & Hersham United FC, and worth checking alongside it.

## About

The club is based at Elm Grove Recreation Ground in Walton and became an FA Charter Standard club in 2019/20. It works with Champion Pro Soccer, led by the former professional Tom Champion, and follows a long-term player development model structured across five age phases.

## Who it is for

Children and young people from age four upwards. Teams run from under-7 through to under-18, and the full range of age groups is being fielded for the 2026/27 season. The club states that its focus is on players of all abilities having the chance to play team football.

## Activities

Youth football, with FA-accredited coaching. The club describes its coaching approach as encouraging decision-making and creativity rather than rigid tactics.

## When and where

Training and matches take place at Elm Grove Recreation Ground. Age-group arrangements are published by the club.

## Joining or taking part

Families should contact the club secretary, Gemma Condron, by email, or use the contact form on the club's own website.
