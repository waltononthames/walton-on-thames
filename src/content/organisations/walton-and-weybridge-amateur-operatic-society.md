Walton & Weybridge Amateur Operatic Society has been staging musicals since 1925 and both performs and rehearses at the Cecil Hepworth Playhouse, the theatre Cecil Hepworth left to the town's dramatic societies.

## About

The society was formed in 1925 and is a registered charity, number 1169732. It is based at the [Cecil Hepworth Playhouse](/things-to-do/cecil-hepworth-playhouse/) in Walton-on-Thames, where its shows are staged and where most rehearsals take place.

## Who it is for

Adults and older teenagers, on stage and off. The society says it is always looking for new members for both performing and crew roles.

## Activities

Two main musical shows a year, plus music hall and compilation shows.

## When and where

Rehearsals are on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with occasional Sundays closer to a performance week, at the Cecil Hepworth Playhouse.

## Joining or taking part

Membership runs at £60 a year for an adult member, £30 for an associate member and a minimum of £90 for a patron. Enquiries go through the society's own website.
