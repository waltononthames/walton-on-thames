## About Patel's Veg and More

Patel's Veg and More is a grocery store on New Zealand Avenue in Walton-on-Thames, stocking authentic Asian groceries alongside fresh fruit, vegetables and speciality food items. The shop describes itself as bringing the diverse tastes of Asia to the heart of Surrey.

It sits in Auckland House, a short walk from The Heart Shopping Centre and Walton town centre.
