The George Inn is a Shepherd Neame pub on the corner of Bridge Street and Thames Street. Its own site lists cask beers, food, live music nights and charity events, and live sport on its screens, including Sky Sports and both racing channels.

There is a small patio garden for drinks outside in summer, and an inglenook fireplace inside.

Opening and food serving times are on [the pub's contact page](https://www.georgeinnwalton.co.uk/contact-us), and live music nights are announced on [its events page](https://www.georgeinnwalton.co.uk/events).
