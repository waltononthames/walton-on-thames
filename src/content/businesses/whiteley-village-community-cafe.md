The Whiteley Village Community Cafe is in the Clubhouse on Octagon Road, [Whiteley Village](/hersham/whiteley-village/), and is signed at the door as The Whiteley Community Café. It opened on 21 July 2025, and the Whiteley Homes Trust describes it as open to residents, the local community and anyone visiting the area.

Opening hours are published on [the Trust's café page](https://www.thewhiteleyhomestrust.org.uk/community-and-activities/community-cafe); check there before you go. Whiteley Village is a private residential community, so keep to the roads and paths on your way in.

**Enquiries:** 01932 842360 is the Whiteley Homes Trust's general enquiries number.
