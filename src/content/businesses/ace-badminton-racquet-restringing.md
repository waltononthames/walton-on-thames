Ace Badminton Club restrings badminton racquets and supplies Yonex rackets. There is no shop: the service is provided at the club's regular sessions, where a resident coach is on hand for practice and restrings.

**Where**

- Heathside Walton-on-Thames, Waterside Drive, Walton-on-Thames, KT12 2JP: Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings
- Cleves School, Oatlands Avenue, Weybridge, KT13 9TS: Monday evenings
