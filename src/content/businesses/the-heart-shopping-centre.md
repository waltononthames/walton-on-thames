The Heart Shopping Centre is the main covered shopping destination in Walton-on-Thames town centre, offering a modern retail environment under one roof.

It brings together a range of high-street shops across fashion, beauty, homewares and food, alongside restaurants and cafes, making it a natural focal point for the town. Walton Library and a gym are also part of the complex, and the centre includes a large public car park.

The central atrium hosts community events through the year, including market stalls and seasonal activities.
