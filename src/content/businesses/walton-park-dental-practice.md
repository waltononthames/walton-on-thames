Walton Park Dental Practice operates on a private basis for adult patients, with NHS treatment offered to children of registered adult patients. It also participates in the Denplan dental payment plan. Services include routine exams and hygiene, Invisalign, fillings, crowns, veneers, implants, whitening and emergency care, from five surgeries.
