## About Khyber Pass

Khyber Pass is an award-winning fine dining Indian restaurant in Walton-on-Thames, bringing together the richness of Indian culinary traditions with a refined, contemporary dining experience. Recognised as Contemporary Indian Restaurant of the Year by the Southern Curry Awards, the restaurant is known for its carefully sourced ingredients, authentic cooking techniques and warm, attentive hospitality.

One of the restaurant's most-loved dishes is the Railway, available with chicken or lamb. Inspired by the first-class railway compartments of India, it features tender meat in a medium-hot caramelised onion sauce, served in a presentation inspired by the classic Indian railway dining experience.

From signature dishes and traditional cooking techniques to thoughtfully designed dining experiences, Khyber Pass offers a distinctive way to discover Indian cuisine in the heart of Walton-on-Thames.
